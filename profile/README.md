**HAMMER GIS** is a GeoTech company combining geographic expertise with software development, systems engineering and professional services, with a specialization in municipalities and the public sector.

From understanding the problem and designing the solution through implementation and ongoing maintenance, we turn geographic information into practical tools for managing infrastructure, assets and workflows, and for supporting informed decisions. Backed by nearly 20 years of hands-on GIS experience.

## Four connected disciplines

| | Discipline | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 🤖 | **GeoAI** | Automated data processing, spatial analysis and quality assurance |
| 💻 | **GeoSoftware** | Custom applications, integrations and automation |
| 🏗️ | **GeoSystems** | GIS systems engineering, design and implementation |
| 🛰️ | **GeoServices** | Mapping and field surveys, drone mapping, orthophoto surveys, CAD-to-GIS conversion, data processing and QA, training and consulting |

## Areas of focus

- **Municipal intelligence** — infrastructure and asset mapping, traffic signage, green spaces, urban hazards
- **CAD-to-GIS** — converting survey and AS-MADE drawings into standardized GIS data
- **Planning** — GIS-based master plans and statutory planning support
- **3D & visual GeoContent** — 3D visualization, maps and aerial imagery
